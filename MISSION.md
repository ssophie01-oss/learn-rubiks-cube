# Mission: Solving a Rubik’s Cube for Fun

## Why
I want to learn how to solve a standard 3×3 Rubik’s Cube because it’s a satisfying personal challenge and a fun party trick. Being able to solve it quickly will give me a sense of accomplishment and allow me to share the skill with friends.

## Success looks like
- I can reliably solve the cube from any scrambled state using the layer‑by‑layer method.
- I can explain the basic notation and the seven core algorithms to a beginner.
- I can complete a solve in under 2 minutes after a few practice sessions.

## Constraints
- I have a few half‑hours per week to practice.
- No need for competition‑level speed; focus on understanding.
- Learning should be incremental and not overwhelming.

## Out of scope
- Advanced speed‑cubing methods (CFOP, Roux, ZZ) beyond the beginner layer‑by‑layer approach.
- Memorizing every possible algorithm for every case.
