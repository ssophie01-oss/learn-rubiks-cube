# Rubik’s Cube Resources

## Knowledge

- [YouTube: J Perm – "How to Solve a Rubik's Cube for Beginners"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7RONKQeQvWQ)
  An excellent video that walks through the full layer‑by‑layer method, explains notation, and shows each algorithm slowly.
- [The Cube – "Beginner Method" written guide](https://www.cubeskills.com/tutorials/beginner-method)
  Step‑by‑step written tutorial with diagrams, perfect for quick reference.
- [Ruwix – Rubik's Cube Notation](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/notation/)
  Comprehensive reference of all standard move symbols and their meanings.
- [AlgDB – "Beginner Method" algorithms](https://algdb.net/puzzle/3x3/algorithms?tags=beginner)
  List of the seven core algorithms used in the beginner method, with visualizations.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Cubers](https://www.reddit.com/r/Cubers/)
  Active Reddit community where beginners ask questions, share tips, and get feedback on solves.
- [Speedsolving Forums – Beginner Section](https://www.speedsolving.com/threads/beginner-method.8792/)
  Forum thread dedicated to newcomers; good place to ask detailed questions.
- Local meetup: Check Meetup.com for "Rubik's Cube" or "Puzzle" groups in your area.

## Gaps
