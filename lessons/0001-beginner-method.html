Rubik’s Cube – Beginner (Layer‑by‑Layer) Method

This lesson walks you through the classic beginner approach to solving a 3×3 cube. It assumes you have a standard cube that turns freely.

1️⃣ Notation

Moves are expressed with single‑letter symbols. A letter alone means a 90° clockwise turn of that face. Adding an apostrophe (') means counter‑clockwise, and a 2 means a 180° turn.

U – Upper face clockwise       U' – Upper face counter‑clockwise
D – Down face clockwise          D' – Down face counter‑clockwise
L – Left face clockwise          L' – Left face counter‑clockwise
R – Right face clockwise         R' – Right face counter‑clockwise
F – Front face clockwise         F' – Front face counter‑clockwise
B – Back face clockwise          B' – Back face counter‑clockwise

Reference: Ruwix notation guide

2️⃣ Steps Overview

  1. White Cross – align white edges with side centres.
  2. First‑layer corners – place the remaining white corners.
  3. Second‑layer edges – insert the four middle‑layer edge pieces.
  4. Yellow cross – form a plus on the opposite (yellow) face.
  5. Orient yellow edges – turn them so side colours match.
  6. Position yellow corners – move each corner to its correct spot.
  7. Orient yellow corners – twist them to finish the cube.

We’ll detail each step with the exact algorithm you need.

3️⃣ Detailed Algorithms

✳️ White cross (no algorithm – just intuitive moves)

Find each white edge, bring it to the U layer, then rotate the U face until the side colour matches the centre on that side.

✳️ First‑layer corners

Algorithm (repeat until the corner is solved):

R U R' U'

✳️ Second‑layer edges

Right‑hand algorithm (when the target slot is on the right):

U R U' R' U' F' U F

Left‑hand algorithm (when the target slot is on the left):

U' L' U L U F U' F'

✳️ Yellow cross

Algorithm (apply until you have a full cross):

F R U R' U' F'

✳️ Orient yellow edges

Algorithm (run until all four edges match side colours):

R U R' U R U2 R'

✳️ Position yellow corners

Algorithm (cycles three corners; repeat until every corner is in the right place):

U R U' L' U R' U' L

✳️ Orient yellow corners

Algorithm for a single mis‑oriented corner (hold it at the front‑right‑up spot):

R' D' R D

Repeat until the corner is solved, then turn the U face to bring the next corner into place.

4️⃣ Practice Routine

  1. Scramble the cube (use any random scramble).
  2. Work through the steps above, **one step at a time**. Don’t rush – focus on understanding why each algorithm works.
  3. After you finish, note how long it took. Try again a few days later and aim to be a few seconds faster.
